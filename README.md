## Kay Du

Hiking creator in Kuala Lumpur, Malaysia. Walking and recording 60 mountains in Malaysia and giving the tracks away.

- **[malaysia-trails-gpx](https://github.com/kaydudrone/malaysia-trails-gpx)** — open CC BY 4.0 GPX dataset of Malaysian hiking trails, recorded on foot. Contribute your track by pull request.
- **[kayducreator](https://github.com/kaydudrone/kayducreator)** — source of [kayducreator.com](https://kayducreator.com): plain HTML/CSS/JS, no framework, free GPX downloads, trail cards, an admin publisher on Vercel.
- Co-founder of [So Hikers Malaysia](https://sohikers.com), a hiking community that has taken 300+ people up Malaysian trails.

Builds everything above with Claude Code. Instagram [@kaydu_3310](https://instagram.com/kaydu_3310).
